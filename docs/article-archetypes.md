# Article Archetypes

This document defines the flexible editorial recipes to create recognizable variety while preserving the brand. Do not invent new structures outside these bounds without updating this document.

## 1. Guide
A comprehensive overview of a biotope or habitat setup.
- **Hero** (full bleed, overlay)
- **Introduction**
- **Foundational concepts** (Prose)
- **`<BentoGrid>`** with Feature Cards
- **Explanatory prose**
- **`<DataTable>` or `<StatStrip>`**
- **`<Timeline>`**
- **`<RankedList>`**
- **Practical conclusion**
- **Sources**

## 2. Species Profile
A detailed breakdown of a specific animal or plant.
- **Hero** (split or minimal)
- **Overview**
- **`<StatStrip>`** (Quick care stats)
- **Habitat and distribution**
- **`<Figure>`** (Annotated image)
- **Care or behavior sections**
- **`<DataTable>`** (Water parameters)
- **`<Callout type="warning">`** (If necessary)
- **Sources**
- **Related species** (using `<BentoGrid cols={3}>`)

## 3. Research Article
Deep dive into a scientific paper or concept.
- **Minimal hero**
- **Research question**
- **Key findings**
- **`<StatStrip>`**
- **Methods and limitations**
- **`<DataTable>`**
- **Interpretation**
- **Sources**

## 4. Comparison Article
Evaluating two or more species, filters, or substrates.
- **Hero**
- **Decision context**
- **Comparison criteria**
- **`<DataTable>`**
- **Detailed prose**
- **`<RankedList>`**
- **Recommendation by use case**
- **Sources**

## 5. Field Story
Narrative-driven account of an expedition or historical figure.
- **Image-led hero**
- **Narrative introduction**
- **Prose and `<Figure>` elements**
- **Pull quote**
- **Contextual research**
- **Closing reflection**
- **Sources**
