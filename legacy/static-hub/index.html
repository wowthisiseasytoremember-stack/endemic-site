Name Etymology

Every scientific name has a story. Travancoricus means "from Travancore." Davidianus honors Father David (Père David's deer). Regani honors Charles Tate Regan. The app surfaces the etymology — and the person — behind every name.

Carinotetraodon travancoricus
"travancoricus" = from Travancore, the former princely state where the Pamba River flows
Betta splendens
Named by Charles Tate Regan, 1910 — "splendens" = shining, splendid

The People Behind the Names

PB

Pieter Bleeker

1819–1878
Dutch army doctor stationed in Indonesia who described 500+ fish from dinner-market specimens. One man named half your Southeast Asian tank.
Chromobotia macracanthus (Clown Loach), Betta spp.
GB

George Albert Boulenger

1858–1937
Described 1,096 fish species — more than anyone in history — without ever traveling. He worked from specimens sent to the British Museum by colonial collectors.
Tetraodon mbu (Giant Puffer), T. miurus (Congo Puffer)
HS

Heinrich Wilhelm Schott

1794–1865
Austrian botanist, director of the Imperial Gardens at Vienna's Schönbrunn Palace. Primary describer of Anthurium — 126 species in our database.
Most Anthurium species trace back to him
