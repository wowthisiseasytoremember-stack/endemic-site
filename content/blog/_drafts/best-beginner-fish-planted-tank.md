## Outline

- **Search intent / hook:** Beginner has a cycled planted tank and wants a safe first stock list. Hook: "the difference between a thriving planted community and a stripped tank comes down to three traits: schooling need, bottom tolerance, and plant appetite." Frame around real r/Aquascape advice, not LFS impulse buys.

- **Section 1 — The three traits that make a fish "planted-tank safe":**
  - Schooling/species behavior: some fish only show their best behavior (schooling, color) when they have cover and group size — plants provide the cover.
  - Bottom compatibility: sand/gravel + root tangles suit bottom dwellers; aggressive bottom fish destroy root systems.
  - Plant appetite: herbivorous/carnivorous mouth mechanics — which fish nip, which ignore, which uproot.
  - State the rule: match fish behavior to what the scape provides, not the other way around.

- **Section 2 — The top picks (species-by-species):**
  - Ember tetra (*Hyphessobrycon amandae*): the poster child. Key evidence point from real advice: they need cover/hiding spots to show proper schooling behavior — open tanks get scattered, nervous fish; planted tanks get tight, glowing schools. Group size (6+ minimum, 10+ ideal), size (~0.8"), temperament, why they're better first tetras than neon for beginners (hardier, less fin-nip drama).
  - Bronze cory (*Corydoras aeneus*): the bottom layer. Real-advice point: corydoras as bottom cleaners — they sort through substrate, eat leftover food, reduce detritus between gravel vacuums. Group of 5+, sand preferred, barbless fins, peaceful. Note: they're not a substitute for maintenance, just a force multiplier.
  - Zebra danio (*Danio rerio*): the survivor. Fast, disease-tolerant, uses the top/mid water column, coexists with both above. Caveat: active swimmers need horizontal length; can be slightly boisterous.
  - Honorable mentions (one line each): white cloud mountain minnow, otosinus (algae, needs established tank), cherry shrimp (ties to the caridina/RO thread from the cost post).

- **Section 3 — Fish to avoid in a beginner planted tank (and why):**
  - Plant eaters: goldfish/fancy gold (size + waste + plant destruction), plecos (suck everything including soft plants), silver dollar (nips carpets).
  - Behavior mismatches: betta with live plants is fine but betta + ember tetra schooling dynamics need care; large barbs that uproot; anything that digs aggressively (some loaches) against delicate root mats.
  - The LFS trap: "community fish" labels that ignore plant appetite and adult size.

- **Section 4 — Stocking a real 16-gal planted community (worked example):**
  - Example build: 12 embers + 5 bronze corys + 4 zebra danios (or swap danios for WCMM) — explain the vertical layering (top/mid/bottom) and why this mix stays peaceful.
  - Add-in order after cycling (link tank-cycling-stalled): small fish first, 1–2 weeks between additions, watch parameters.
  - Water parameter note: all three picks tolerate standard freshwater; if running RO + remineralization for future shrimp, these all handle it fine.
