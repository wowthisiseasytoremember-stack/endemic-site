# E — The Pink Princess Patent Story Is Backwards
## Corrected source pack

## Safe core story

The old article premise was wrong: USPP31149 is not a Pink Princess patent.

Primary records:
- USPP31149 — *Metasequoia glyptostroboides* 'Urban Spire'
  - https://patents.google.com/patent/USPP31149P2/en
- USPP36881 — *Philodendron erubescens* 'EM0003'
  - https://patents.google.com/patent/USPP36881P2/en
  - describes EM0003 as a naturally occurring whole-branch mutation of Pink Princess
  - explicitly calls Pink Princess unpatented
- USPP37308 — *Philodendron* 'BVCL01'
  - https://patents.google.com/patent/USPP37308P2/en
  - independently describes Pink Princess as not patented
